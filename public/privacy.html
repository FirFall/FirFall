Privacy Policy

This policy describes what FirFall actually does with your data. It is written from the software itself rather than from a template, because FirFall is a small self-hosted project and a generic policy full of things it does not do would be worse than none.

The short version

FirFall has

  • No advertising, and no ad network or analytics script from any company.
  • No third-party trackers, pixels, or fingerprinting.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data for money.
  • No readable copy of your IP address. Ever.

FirFall does

  • Keep what it needs to run the site: your account, your uploads, your comments.
  • Send the parts it does not store itself to Cloudflare and Backblaze, which host the servers and the video files.
  • Filter uploads and comments automatically, and log a blocked attempt against the account that made it.

What is stored

DataWhy it exists
UsernameIt is your identity on the site. It is public.
PasswordNever stored. Only a salted hash of it. FirFall cannot read your password, and neither can anyone who gets hold of the database.
Channel bio, banner, profile pictureYours to set and change at any time in FirFall Studio. Public.
Join date, account role, ban statusRunning the site and moderating it.
IP addressOnly ever as a salted one-way fingerprint (HMAC-SHA256). The address itself is not written down. Used to sign you in, to enforce a ban, and to keep a network-banned account out. A moderator sees a 12-character prefix and nothing more.
Session tokenKept in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in. A copy is kept on the server so the token can be checked.
Videos and embersThe file itself, plus its title, description, filename, size, type, length, visibility, view count and thumbnail. Public unless you set a video to unlisted or private.
Comments and repliesThe text, who wrote it, which video it is on, and who it replies to. Public.
Likes, hearts, subscriptions, watch history, notification recordsThe features themselves - they are how the site knows what you have liked and what you follow.
View and watch statisticsFor the creator's own Studio analytics: per video, per day, how many views and how far people got. The viewer is a random number generated by your browser, not your account, so this works for signed-out visitors and does not become a record of who you are.
Reports, bans, appealsModeration. If you report something or appeal a ban, that is kept with your username.

What is not stored

  • Your password. Only a hash, as above.
  • Your IP address in readable form. Only the fingerprint.
  • Your email address. FirFall has no email field and cannot contact you directly - which is also why there is no "forgot password" email, only a moderator reset.
  • Anything from an ad or analytics company. There are none.

Who else sees your data

FirFall runs on infrastructure it does not own. Those providers receive only what their part of the job needs:

ProviderWhat it handles
CloudflareRuns the code, hosts the database, stores video files, and classifies video thumbnails with an image-recognition model so a clean title with an unsuitable picture still gets caught.
BackblazeReceives video files uploaded directly from your device, and serves them back for playback.

Video files are handed out through short-lived signed links rather than public URLs, so a link to a file stops working on its own.

How long things are kept

  • Videos, comments and your account stay up until you delete them, or until a moderator removes them for breaking the Community Guidelines.
  • Deleting a video removes the file and its thumbnail from storage, not just the row that points at them.
  • Watch statistics are kept per day and are not linked to an account.
  • Records of blocked upload attempts are kept as long as the account that made them.

One honest gap: signing out clears the token on your device, but the server-side session record is not deleted at that moment. On a shared device, signing out is therefore not as thorough as it looks - the safest thing is to sign in and out on your own device only.

Your choices

  • Change or remove your bio and pictures at any time in FirFall Studio.
  • Change who can see a video - public, unlisted or private - from Manage videos in Studio.
  • Delete a video whenever you like; the file goes with it.
  • Report a video or comment you think breaks the rules.
  • Appeal a ban from the ban screen, which still works when you cannot sign in.
  • Delete your account yourself. There is a Delete account button on your own channel page, and in the account view on the phone. See below.

Deleting your account

You do not have to email anybody or ask politely. The Delete account button is on your own channel page (and in the account view in the Android app). Pressing it opens a screen that says exactly what will be removed, and asks for a reason — which is optional, and which nobody will hold against you if you leave it blank.

What happens next

  • Your request goes to the admins with the reason, if you gave one.
  • An admin can confirm it, and your account is deleted then.
  • If no admin gets to it, it is deleted automatically after three days anyway. A check runs once a day, and also whenever an admin opens the queue. You are not left waiting on a volunteer.
  • You can cancel at any point before then. The channel page and the app both show a Cancel button while a request is open, and cancelling stops the deletion. Nothing is deleted until the window is up or an admin confirms.

While a request is open, your account keeps working exactly as it did. You are not signed out early, and nothing disappears in the meantime.

What is deleted

Everything attached to the account, not just the login:

  • Your account, your session, and your profile picture and banner files.
  • Every video and ember you uploaded, and the actual video and thumbnail files in storage - not just the rows pointing at them.
  • Every comment and reply you wrote.
  • Your likes, the hearts you gave, your subscriptions (in both directions), your watch history and your notification records.
  • Your reports, and any appeal you had open.

Deleting your comments removes them from other people's threads rather than leaving them as an anonymous stub. If a reply you wrote was under someone else's comment, that reply disappears too.

The two exceptions

  • Moderator and admin accounts cannot be deleted this way. On a site run by a small number of people, a self-service deletion that can remove the last administrator is a site with no administrator. Another admin has to do it by hand.
  • Backups. Files are removed from live storage when your account is deleted. Backups taken by the hosting providers for disaster recovery are outside FirFall's control and age out on their own schedule.

Children

FirFall is not directed at children under 13, and it has no age verification and no parental controls. Do not create an account for a child. If you believe a child has created one, contact the operator and it will be removed along with its content.

Security

Passwords are salted and hashed before storage. Sessions use long random tokens. IP addresses are fingerprinted rather than kept. Media is served from signed, expiring links. None of this makes a small volunteer-run site bulletproof - if you find a way in, please report it rather than using it.

Changes to this policy

If FirFall starts collecting something new, this page changes and the date at the top changes with it. The previous behaviour is described here rather than quietly replaced.
