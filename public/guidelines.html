FirFall is a small, self-hosted video site. It is not a public utility with a staffed trust and safety team - it is run by a handful of volunteers - so these rules are short on purpose, and the enforcement behind them is mostly automated. Read the filters section: knowing what is caught automatically tells you a lot about what is not.

The rules

Do not post:

  • Sexual content involving minors, or anything that could be read as it. This is not a warning, it is an instant permanent ban, and it is reported to the authorities where the law requires it.
  • Hate speech or harassment - slurs, or targeting someone for who they are, their background, their gender, their sexuality, or a disability.
  • Credible threats, or content that praises or glorifies violence against people.
  • Personal information about someone else - a home address, a phone number, a workplace, a licence plate, a bank detail. Doxxing gets accounts removed.
  • Content you do not have the right to upload. FirFall has no licence to broadcast someone else's work. If it is not yours, do not post it.
  • Spam, scams, or bulk identical posts. This includes engagement farming, fake giveaways, and crypto or gift-card bait.
  • Impersonating another person, a channel, or FirFall itself.
  • Nudity or graphic gore outside a clearly labelled context. FirFall has no age gate, so assume anything you post here can be seen by anyone, including a nine-year-old with a phone.
  • CSAM, malware, or instructions for making something dangerous.

In the comments

The same standard applies. Disagree with someone's video all you like; do not go after the person. There is a Report button on every comment, and replies only ever go one level deep - a reply to a reply becomes a sibling on purpose, so a thread cannot be used to bury a conversation.

Creator hearts are not a licence

Only the person who uploaded a video can heart a comment on it. That is a small acknowledgement, not moderation, and it does not make anything allowed. The filters and the report queue do not care who posted it.

What the filters catch on their own

Every username, video title, description, filename, ember name and comment is checked against a keyword list the moment it is submitted. Two things happen depending on what matches:

  • Blocked Matches for severe prohibited content, racism or extremism. The upload never reaches storage, and the attempt is logged against the account.
  • Held Matches for adult content or gore. The file is discarded and the upload is held for a human to look at. It is not published while it waits.

Thumbnails are additionally run through an image classifier so that a video with a clean title and an unsuitable picture does not slip through.

These filters catch the obvious cases. They are keyword and image matching, not understanding, and they will miss plenty. They are also not a promise of instant removal - if something gets through, the report button is how it comes down.

Reporting something

Every video and every comment has a Report button. Pick the reason that fits best and add detail if you have it - a specific line or a timestamp is worth more than "this is bad".

Reports go into a queue that moderators work through. You can report the same thing twice and it will only be counted once, but you can report two different videos. You will not be told who made the report, and neither will the person reported.

What happens when something breaks the rules

Content

A video or comment can be held for review and then removed. A held upload tells you it was held and why, and the file is discarded in the meantime.

Accounts

An account can be suspended or banned. A ban always carries a stated reason - a ban screen that will not tell you why is a bug, and if you get one, appeal it.

Appealing

A banned account cannot sign in, but the ban screen has an appeal form that still works. Write what you think went wrong - moderators read these, and they can lift a ban and leave a note on it. One open appeal per account, so the inbox stays readable.

If you want to leave

There is a Delete account button on your own channel page, and in the account view in the Android app. You can say why if you want to; nobody will hold it against you, and leaving the box empty is completely fine.

Your request goes to the admins, who can confirm it. If nobody gets to it within three days it is deleted anyway — you are not left waiting on a volunteer having a free afternoon. And you can cancel the request at any point before that happens; nothing is removed until either an admin confirms or the three days are up.

Leaving takes your videos, your comments, your profile and your account with it. It is not a way to keep a channel while dropping the parts you regret, and it cannot be undone. See the Privacy Policy for the full list of what goes.

Ownership and takedown

If something you posted has been removed and you think that was wrong, appeal it. If someone else has posted your work, report the upload and say that you own it - a moderator can take the video down and ban the account that took it.

Privacy, briefly

FirFall has no advertising and no third-party trackers. IP addresses are never stored in readable form - only as a keyed fingerprint. There is a full Privacy Policy that says exactly what is kept.
